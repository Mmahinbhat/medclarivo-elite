Delete your MedClarivo Elite account

You can permanently delete your MedClarivo Elite account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need to contact us first.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open the MedClarivo Elite app, or sign in on our website.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom bar), which opens Settings. Parents: open Settings on the parent dashboard.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. If you signed in with Google, Apple or your phone number, type DELETE instead.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Can't sign in? Ask us by email

Email official@medclarivo.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm it's you. We complete requests within 30 days. Mentor and staff accounts are also deleted this way.

Parents or guardians of a student under 18 can ask us to delete the student's account the same way.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, password and picture
  • Your study data: progress, test results, study time, streaks, flashcards and goals
  • Your doubts and replies, messages, call history and call recordings, mentor sessions, session notes and evaluations
  • Your notification settings and the devices registered for notifications
  • Your phone sign-in with our verification provider, and your "Sign in with Apple" link

What we keep

  • Safety reports you sent through "Report a Concern", kept for safety and legal reasons
  • Records of actions taken by our staff (audit logs)

See our Privacy Policy for details.
