Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

MedClarivo Elite ("MedClarivo", "we", "us") is a mentorship and study app for students preparing for NEET and other medical entrance exams. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the MedClarivo Elite apps for iPhone and Android and to our website.

The short version
  • We collect what we need to run your account, your study progress and your mentorship.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

MedClarivo Elite is operated from India by Mohammad Mahin Bhat, the developer of the app. You can reach us at official@medclarivo.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash), and an optional profile picture link. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from them.
  • Study profile: answers you give during onboarding, such as your target exam, class or level, institution, daily study hours, and previous and target scores.
  • Your activity in the app: chapters and progress, test and quiz answers and scores, study time, streaks and points, flashcard progress, and daily goals.
  • Communication with mentors: messages, doubts you post and replies, mentor session bookings, and the notes, evaluations and feedback your mentor records about your sessions.
  • Safety reports and support requests you send through "Report a Concern" or to our email.
  • Parent or guardian accounts: if a parent account is linked to a student, we store that link so the parent can see the student's progress and mentor feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Voice calls: who called whom, when, and how long the call lasted, and an audio recording of the call (see section 5).
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for your device, or a browser push subscription if you use the website.
  • Security data: your IP address when you request a phone verification code (to limit abuse), failed sign-in attempts, and basic device information sent by your browser or phone.

The app stores your sign-in session and theme choice on your own device. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email or phone, and sign you in.
  • To run the study features: tests, progress tracking, analytics about your own performance, and recommendations about weak areas.
  • To connect you with a mentor, and let you message, call and hold sessions with them.
  • To let a linked parent or guardian follow your progress.
  • To send you notifications and emails about your account, sessions and replies. You can turn off optional notifications in Settings.
  • To review call recordings for the quality of mentoring and the safety of students.
  • To keep the service safe, investigate reports, and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Who we share it with

Your mentor (and their assistant, if they have one) can see your profile, progress and the conversations you have with them. A linked parent or guardian can see your progress and mentor feedback, but not your mentor's private notes. Our team can access account data when needed to provide support, handle safety reports or keep the service running.

5. Call recordings

Every voice call in MedClarivo is recorded, so we can check the quality of mentoring and keep students safe. Both people on the call see a "This call is recorded" notice while it is being recorded. If you don't want to be recorded, please don't use in-app calls, and message your mentor instead.

  • Who can listen: only MedClarivo's admin team. Mentors, students and parents cannot play recordings in the app. Every time an admin plays or deletes a recording, it is logged.
  • How long we keep them: recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Where they're stored: in private cloud storage (see section 4), never shared publicly.
  • If you delete your account, recordings of calls you were on are deleted too.

6. Students under 18

Many students preparing for NEET are under 18. If you are under 18, please use MedClarivo with the knowledge and permission of your parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can request a parent account linked to their child, review the child's progress, and ask us to access, correct or delete the child's data by emailing official@medclarivo.com. We do not show ads to anyone, and we do not use students' data for advertising or behavioural tracking.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Call recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile and the data linked to it, as described on our account deletion page. Safety reports and our internal audit logs are kept for safety and legal reasons.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your profile in Settings, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account, or see other ways to delete.
  • Turn off optional email and push notifications in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Withdraw consent or raise a concern about how we handle your data by emailing us.

We reply to privacy requests within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Sign-in sessions expire and can be ended on every device from Settings. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app or by email.

11. Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about your data, email official@medclarivo.com. We'll acknowledge it promptly and respond within 30 days.
